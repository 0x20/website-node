Teus will present his 3D-printed mushroom light and walk us through the design decisions behind it. 

This session will “reverse engineer” the project: starting from the visible object and working inward to understand the technical and practical constraints that shaped it.

Topics will include:

- Mechanical design of the PLA-printed enclosure (diffusion, wall thickness, assembly)
- Power architecture (USB-C choice, current limits, safety considerations)
- Electronics layout (ESP32, LED selection, thermal considerations)
- Firmware decisions (WLED configuration, networking, user control)
- Trade-offs between aesthetics, manufacturability, and maintainability

Expect a hands-on, constraint-driven breakdown of how a decorative object becomes a robust, rechargeable, Wi-Fi-enabled light fixture.

Open discussion and questions encouraged.
