# Bring Your Own Meat BBQ 🔥

We light the big grill. You bring stuff to put on it.

Bring your **meat / veggies / weird grill experiments**, plus **your own buns, sauces, and drinks**. Sharing is nice but not mandatory.

If you feel like contributing: flip things, help clean a bit, or help move the giant grill back inside afterwards so Qwaxis doesn’t break his back again.

Smoke, food, and hacker chatter guaranteed.
